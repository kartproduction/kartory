# Kartory

Illustrated books that pair art with social issues such as race, displacement, and kindness. By [Kart Production](https://kartproduction.github.io/kart/).

**Website:** https://kartproduction.github.io/kartory/

> **Free to read, not to copy.** The books are free to download as PDFs. Please don't sell, re-upload, or change them. See [License](#license).

---

## About

*Change begins here.*

Kartory aims to tell stories that make the truth impossible to look away from. The goal is a growing catalogue of young-adult and picture books where the art carries much of the story.

## The books

| Book | Year | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Silence in the World: Undefined** | 2019 | Illustrated nonfiction on racial injustice and systemic racism in the U.S., written to open conversations people often avoid. |
| **The Kindness Wonderland** | 2024 | A picture book about a young refugee girl who learns how small acts of compassion spread hope. |
| **Visual Land** | 2019 | A collection of photographs paired with quotes, made to spark imagination. |
| **Baby Boy** | 2019 | A children's picture book about a baby cat who spreads kindness. |

All four titles are free to download as PDFs, with no account required.
